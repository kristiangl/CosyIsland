# Sound effects

Built by `tools/make_sfx.sh`. Played by the `Sfx` autoload, by name: `chop` plays one of
`chop_1.ogg`, `chop_2.ogg`... at random.

- Synthesised with ffmpeg by the script: the ambience loops (`rain_loop`, `wind_loop`,
  `waves_loop`, `stream_loop`, `crickets_loop`), `bird_*`, `thunder_*`, `splash`, `plop`,
  `boing`, `sparkle`, `munch`, `rustle`, `rumble`, `whoosh_*` and the `voice_*` blips
  characters babble in.
- Everything else comes from Kenney's audio packs (Interface Sounds, Impact Sounds, RPG
  Audio, Music Jingles), CC0, www.kenney.nl.
